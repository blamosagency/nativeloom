THE PROBLEM

A translated message isn't always a message that connects.

A brand's tone, references, and cultural context can get lost across borders. Our vision is an AI-native localization workspace that preserves brand identity while adapting expression to the audience.

OUR DIRECTION

Built for brands crossing borders.

We're exploring a simpler way for independent brands and small teams to launch into new markets without losing the voice that makes them recognizable.

The first planned product focuses on multilingual marketing copy, voice consistency, and side-by-side localization. Claude is the intended language-reasoning layer for a future working MVP.
